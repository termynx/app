TERMYNX / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy describes how Termynx handles information when you use the Termynx Android app or this public website. It does not describe the privacy practices of servers you connect to or other websites you visit.

Information involved in using Termynx

  • Account information. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, Termynx receives information needed to identify your account, such as your name, email address, profile image, and sign-in provider.
  • Server and connection information. The app stores the server profiles, connection details, credentials, and SSH keys you choose to add on your device. Terminal sessions and file transfers connect to the remote hosts you select.
  • Optional cloud sync. If you enable cloud sync, selected server profiles, credentials, and SSH keys are encrypted on your device before being stored by the Termynx service. The service also processes account and sync metadata needed to operate the feature.
  • Usage and diagnostics. The Android app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Google Analytics to measure app usage and feature performance. Events can include screen views, sign-ins, file-transfer actions, ad interactions, and connection events. Connection events can include a server name and port.
  • Advertising data. When ads are shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process an IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic data for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

How information is used and shared

Termynx uses account information to provide sign-in and account features; server information to make the connections you request; optional cloud data to sync your workspace; and usage data to understand and improve the app. Information is shared with your chosen remote servers when you connect or transfer files, with Google or GitHub when you use their sign-in services, and with Google for analytics or ads as described above. The Termynx service receives information required for account and optional sync features.

Your choices

You can use local server features without enabling cloud sync. You can turn cloud sync off and remove local server profiles or keys in the app. Turning sync off or uninstalling the app does not by itself delete information already stored with your Termynx account. You can manage your Android advertising ID in device settings.

Security and storage

Termynx uses device security features to protect locally stored credentials and keys. Optional cloud-sync content is encrypted on the device before upload. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. The remote servers you connect to are outside Termynx's control.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it or clear the app's data. Account information and optional cloud-sync data may remain with the Termynx service while your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated cloud data, email termynx@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We may need to verify that the request is yours and will explain any information that must be retained for legal or security reasons.

This website

This public website does not include app analytics scripts, advertising scripts, or a contact form. If it is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address when serving the page under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Termynx changes. The date above shows when this page was last revised. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact termynx@gmail.com.
